## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of haute couture and biometric intelligence ("Technology is the New Black"). It caters to discerning tastemakers, avant-garde technologists, and luxury health enthusiasts who view biometric integration not as medical apparatus, but as an expression of personal sovereignty and style.

The aesthetic direction is **Dark Luxury Cyber-Couture**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian layers, velvety midnight tones, and cinematic shadows derived from conductive textiles and silicon substrates.
- **Accents:** Luminous electric circuit cyan paired with subtle metallic champagne gold traces, reminiscent of embedded golden circuitry running along premium garments.
- **Visual Voice:** Minimalist, mysterious, ultra-precise, and editorial. The interface maintains generous negative space, crisp micro-borders, and disciplined optical weight.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly in dark mode, reflecting the rich textures of conductive smart textiles and integrated circuit silicon:
- **Primary (`#00E5FF`)**: Electric circuit cyan used for active state telemetry, key interaction triggers, and precision data metrics.
- **Secondary (`#00B4D8`)**: Deep conductive cyan for secondary indicators, progress fills, and supporting visual hierarchy.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`)**: Refined cyber-gold/champagne metallic for luxury tier badges, premium hardware status, and biometric equilibrium states.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F15`)**: Deep obsidian base canvas. Surface layers elevate to `#121824` with translucent glass finishes (`surface-glass`).
- **Text & Accents**: Pure stark white (`#F8FAFC`) anchors display typography, supported by cool slate (`#94A3B8`) for telemetry secondary labels.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts the structural, technical precision of **Space Grotesk** with the refined, fluid ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:
- **Space Grotesk** is deployed across editorial hero headlines, telemetry numerical values, section tags, and navigational labels. In small sizes, uppercase tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) imparts an architectural luxury feel.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides supreme legibility for descriptive editorial passages, technical garment specifications, and continuous health streams without visual fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric luxury editorial grid fused with fluid cybernetic dashboard structures:
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 48px margins and 24px gutters. Wide hero spans interlock full-bleed garment imagery with tight technical stat clusters.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 32px margins and 20px gutters. Content balances editorial focus with double-column sensor metrics.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 20px margins and 16px gutters. Collapses into layered vertical cards and docked bottom controls.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict multiples of 4px/8px, prioritizing wide airy gaps between narrative sections (`space-xl` and higher) while maintaining micro-density in biometric telemetry clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through frosted glassmorphism, directional cyan backlights, and precise luminous outlines rather than traditional drop shadows:
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0F15` deep obsidian base canvas.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Shells):** Semi-opaque `#121824` with 1px border (`border-subtle`).
- **Level 2 (Glassmorphic Cards):** `rgba(18, 24, 36, 0.65)` layered over backdrop-filter `blur(16px)` with hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Active / Focused (Luminous Elevation):** Outer glow `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.18)` paired with an active edge stroke (`#00E5FF`).
- **Circuit Gold Accents:** Used sparingly for premium status rings and device connection confirmations with a soft warmth glow `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines high-tech capsule geometries with structured corners:
- Standard interactive containers, biometric cards, and telemetry viewports employ `roundedness: 2` (8px base, 16px `rounded-lg`) to balance futuristic circuitry with ergonomic grace.
- Navigation pills, biometric status indicators, filter tags, and primary CTAs utilize full capsule radiuses (`9999px`) to echo the curvature of the human silhouette and wearable hardware housings.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Circuit Action:** Full pill capsule (`rounded-full`), solid `#00E5FF` fill with stark `#0B0F15` Space Grotesk bold text. On hover: subtle ambient aura glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Ghost / Couturier:** Pill border in `border-subtle` or hairline cyan, transparent background, `#F8FAFC` label. On hover: background shifts to `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Gold Luxury Tier CTA:** Champagne gold gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFB703, #D4AF37)`) with obsidian typography for bespoke garment reservations and smart hardware unlock sequences.

### Glassmorphic Cards & Telemetry Pods
- **Garment & Metric Cards:** Built with `surface-glass`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Hovering states elevate the card through a transition to `border-luminous` (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`).
- Integrated technical metadata stamps (e.g. `[ SYNCED // 98.4 BPM ]`) sit at card corners in uppercase `label-sm`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Compact pill-shaped modules containing a 6px pulsing LED dot (`#00E5FF` or `#D4AF37`) alongside an uppercase `label-sm` font.
- Used for fabric sensor modes (e.g., `HAPTIC READY`, `BODY-HEAT GENERATING`, `BIOSYNC`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input surfaces are inset obsidian fields (`#080C10`) with thin 1px boundaries. On focus, the boundary shifts to `#00E5FF` with a subtle inner and outer luminous glow.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons feature sharp, micro-beveled cybernetic geometries with electric cyan dot/check indicators.

### Navigation & Editorial App Bar
- Floating glassmorphic dock anchored at screen edges with frosted pill geometry (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- Menu items utilize tracked Space Grotesk with cyan micro-dots marking the active category.
