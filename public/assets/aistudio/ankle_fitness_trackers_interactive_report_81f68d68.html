Why the Supramalleolar Ankle Outperforms the Wrist in Wearable Health Monitoring

Why Ankle PPG Outperforms Cuff-Based ABI (68.1% vs. 19.1%)

In patients with diabetes or chronic kidney disease, medial arterial calcification renders tibial arteries non-compressible, causing false-negative Ankle-Brachial Index (ABI) cuff readings. Optical PPGratio (pulsatile AC to non-pulsatile DC ratio) measures microvascular perfusion directly without requiring arterial compression, while also capturing orthostatic venoarteriolar reflexes (~80 mmHg hydrostatic column).

Sensitivity, Specificity, and Overall Accuracy for Parkinson's Freezing of Gait (FoG) and gait impairment.

Closed-Loop FoG Intervention -1.8s Lead Time

In advanced Parkinson's Disease, Freezing of Gait manifests as high-frequency lower-limb trembling (3–8 Hz) compared to normal locomotion (0.5–3 Hz). Time-series models detect subtle stride-length decay up to 1.8 seconds before an actual freeze occurs—triggering rhythmic haptic or auditory cues at the ankle to bypass basal ganglia deficits and prevent forward falls.

Bilateral Stroke & Arthroplasty Symmetry

Wrist sensors provide zero insight into inter-limb coordination. Dual ankle accelerometers quantify hemiparetic swing-time deficits, circumduction, and vertical impact asymmetry between paretic and non-paretic limbs to guide neuroplasticity-focused physical therapy.
